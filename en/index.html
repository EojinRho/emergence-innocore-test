About

From robots whose form determined their function to robots that adapt on their own

The Emergent Intelligence Physical AI Research Center (EIPAI) is an InnoCORE research center led by DGIST, with the Korea Institute of Machinery & Materials (KIMM) as its partner institution. The project title is “Emergent Intelligence-based Physical AI Robot Implementation and Cross-domain Validation.”

Conventional robots were built by layering function and then intelligence onto a fixed form. The Center studies emergent intelligence robots whose structure, behavior, and intelligence evolve together through physical interaction, and validates the same technology in both industrial and medical settings.
