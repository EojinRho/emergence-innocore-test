About

From robots whose form determined their function to robots that adapt on their own

The Emergent Intelligence Physical AI Research Center is an InnoCORE research center led by DGIST, with the Korea Institute of Machinery & Materials (KIMM) as its partner institution. The project title is “Emergent Intelligence-based Physical AI Robot Implementation and Cross-domain Validation.”

Conventional robots were built by layering function and then intelligence onto a fixed form. The Center studies emergent intelligence robots whose structure, behavior, and intelligence evolve together through physical interaction, and validates the same technology in both industrial and medical settings.

Join Us

We are recruiting 10 InnoCORE Fellows (postdoctoral researchers). Fellows are not assigned to a single lab: they rotate through the five subprojects and take part in the full research cycle, from core technology through application to validation.

Rotation across subprojects

Rather than staying in one field, Fellows rotate between subprojects and work with the research groups on an ongoing basis. Three of the Fellows are seconded to KIMM to conduct industrial validation research.

Multi-mentoring

Fellows are mentored jointly by DGIST departments (emergent intelligence and system integration), KIMM (industrial and medical robot hardware), DGIST research divisions (medical robotics and applications), and leading international scholars at overseas partner institutions.

Research support

Fellows have access to an integrated research space and experimental infrastructure (GPU servers, testbeds) and receive support for international academic activities and overseas research exchanges. Administrative and research support systems let them focus on their research.
