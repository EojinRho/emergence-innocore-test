# emergence-innocore-test

Test draft of the DGIST InnoCORE Emergent Intelligence Physical AI Research Center website (창발지능 Physical AI 연구단).

- Korean pages at the root, English pages in `en/`; the KO/EN switch is at the top right.
- All content lives in `assets/data.js` (Korean) and `assets/data.en.js` (English overrides); `assets/app.js` renders it.
- Static files only: no build step.

This is a review draft. Figures, the member list, photos and profiles are not final and are published here for internal review only.
